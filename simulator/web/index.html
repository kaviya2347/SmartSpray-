Mission briefing

A survey-and-spray drone treats a tomato field plant by plant. Watch it work on its own, or press M at any time to fly it yourself; press M again and the autopilot re-plans from where you left it.

  1. Place markers. The drone flies to each corner the operator marked and drops an ArUco marker.
  2. Map the field. From 15 m it photographs the whole field, reads the four markers to fix the boundary and size, and finds every plant.
  3. Scan plants. Flying low, it photographs each plant. Every photo goes to the disease model (EfficientNetB0), then severity and the dosage rules.
  4. Plan route. Healthy and mild plants are skipped. It works out the shortest route over the plants that need spray, split into tank loads.
  5. Spray. It flies the route and sprays each target with its own dose.
  6. Return & land. Flight log and savings report.
Field

Synthetic field: every plant is a real PlantVillage test photo the model never saw in training. The drone and its sensors are simulated; the detection, disease model, severity, rules and route planner are the real SmartSpray modules.
